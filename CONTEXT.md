# Story Bearings

A static, spoiler-safe public website that helps readers of dense novels keep their bearings without revealing what they have not yet read.

## Language

**Story Bearings**:
The product — a public website of design-time Book data, with no AI at request time.
_Avoid_: companion app, study guide, SparkNotes (when naming this product)

**Book**:
A novel the site covers, authored into the repository at design time.
_Avoid_: title, work (when meaning a covered novel)

**Division**:
A part or the epilogue of a Book, containing ordered chapters.
_Avoid_: section, book (when meaning a part)

**Person**:
A key figure in a Book. Walk-ons are not Persons; they appear only in orientation prose.
_Avoid_: character, cast member (when meaning this record)

**Reader**:
A first-time reader of a Book, mid-Book, using Story Bearings as a sidecar to stay oriented so they can keep enjoying the Book.
_Avoid_: user, student, reviewer (when meaning this person)

**Spoiler-safe**:
Containing only what the Book's text has already shown or said through the Reader's Place, on every surface that claims to be spoiler-safe.
_Avoid_: spoiler-free

**Place**:
The chapter the Reader has read through: a part-and-chapter, or epilogue-and-chapter, of a Book.
_Avoid_: progress, bookmark, chapter number (alone)

**Bearings**:
The spoiler-safe page at a Place: part-and-chapter label, orientation prose, and Roster.
_Avoid_: recap, study guide, chapter summary (when naming this page)

**Roster**:
The spoiler-safe list of key people on Bearings, through the Reader's Place.
_Avoid_: cast, dramatis personae (when meaning this list)

**Complete roster**:
The static, place-agnostic page of the Book's key cast and final roles. Opening it is opting out of spoiler-safe Bearings.
_Avoid_: roster (when meaning this page)
